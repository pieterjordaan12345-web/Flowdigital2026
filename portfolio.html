Siphiminenhle (PTY) Ltd

Website Design & Digital Accessibility

Challenge

Siphiminenhle (PTY) Ltd had no website, which made it difficult for people to understand what they do, find information quickly, or get in touch. Most communication had to happen manually, which slowed everything down.

Solution

I built a clean, simple, and fast website that clearly explains their services and purpose in a way anyone can understand. It includes instant contact options like email, WhatsApp, and phone call buttons so users can reach them immediately.

Result

The organisation now has a professional online presence that is easy to use, easy to understand, and fully accessible. The entire system was built using pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — making it lightweight, fast, and reliable. It was also delivered at a low-cost, no-hassle setup, giving them everything they needed without complexity or delays.
